Plan on roughly 10–15 minutes with current company facts. Do not enter sensitive employee case data. A work email is required for delivery of the complete PDF advisory. REPORT GLIMPSE

Enough to understand the insight. Not the full report.

The on-site result is intentionally concise. It confirms the executive position, the main issue and the first actions. Detailed findings, source-by-source evidence, methodology, scenarios and the complete action plan remain in the emailed PDF.
