CHANGE INTELLIGENCE

A later analysis should tell you what changed — not just generate another report.

The first confirmed assessment becomes a baseline. Later analyses can compare company facts and deterministic findings so the reason behind a changed recommendation remains visible.

How the loop works

  1. Complete a specialist analysis and create a confirmed company baseline.
  2. Return when strategy, workforce, locations, structure or other material facts change.
  3. Update only what changed.
  4. GrowWithHR compares the new facts and resulting findings against the prior baseline.

What Change Intelligence should distinguish

  • Company-fact change: the company changed.
  • Finding change: the same rule now produces a different result.
  • Methodology change: a rule/framework version changed.
  • Information change: a previous unknown became known, or vice versa.

This distinction prevents a new report from looking different without explaining why.
