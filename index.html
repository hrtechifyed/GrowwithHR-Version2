WHAT THIS MEANS FOR A BUYER

You can see how the recommendation was reached.

Open the source and see which company facts and evaluation criteria were used. The goal is to make the basis easy to review.

  • Sources are named. Evidence is not hidden behind “AI says”.
  • The reasoning is explained. GrowWithHR explains how it interpreted the information.
  • Missing facts remain visible. The product can say it needs more information.
  • Organization analysis does not score people. It evaluates how the organization is structured and works.
