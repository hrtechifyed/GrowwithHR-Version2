WHY THE ANSWER IS TRACEABLE

The recommendation and its source are kept separate.

A public source can support a principle without being responsible for the exact recommendation GrowWithHR gives your company. GrowWithHR makes that distinction visible.

01

Capture explicit facts

The analysis starts with facts you supplied or clearly derived metrics.

02

Apply a versioned rule

The GrowWithHR rule explains how those facts produced the result.

03

Show the source basis

The relevant public source explains the underlying principle or authority.

04

Keep uncertainty visible

Missing facts, assumptions and specialist-review boundaries stay part of the answer.

WHAT THIS MEANS FOR A BUYER

You can challenge the recommendation.

Open the source, inspect the rule and see which company facts were used. The point is not to make the system sound certain; it is to make the basis reviewable.

  • Sources are named. Evidence is not hidden behind “AI says”.
  • Rules are disclosed. GrowWithHR takes responsibility for its interpretation.
  • Missing facts remain visible. The product can say it needs more information.
  • Organization analysis does not score people. It evaluates structural operating patterns.
