How Organization Structure recommendations are grounded

Every Organization Structure finding separates two things: the public source that supports the organization-design principle, and the GrowWithHR rule that interprets your company facts. This is designed so you can see exactly where a recommendation came from.

GrowWithHR Organization Structure Assessment Framework v1.0

The framework is a free public methodology. GrowWithHR uses deterministic rules rather than an AI model to decide the structural status shown in a finding.

Public sources do not endorse GrowWithHR. They support the underlying principles used in the analysis.

Important threshold disclosure

Where a finding uses a numeric watch or action trigger, the report tells you whether that number is a GrowWithHR prototype trigger. We do not present those values as an industry benchmark unless a source actually publishes that benchmark.

How to read a finding: “Source basis” tells you what public guidance supports the principle. “GrowWithHR rule” tells you how GrowWithHR converted the facts you supplied into Stable, Watch, Action, or Needs information.

What GrowWithHR does not claim

Organization Structure does not score people, judge individual manager capability, recommend dismissals, determine compensation, or claim that a public source prescribed GrowWithHR's prototype thresholds. The result is structural guidance based on the facts supplied and the disclosed rule set.
