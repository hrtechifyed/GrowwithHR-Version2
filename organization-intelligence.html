What this analysis does

The Organization Structure & Growth Engine evaluates structural facts and operating patterns. It does not score people, assess individual capability, recommend dismissals, determine compensation, or decide legal applicability. “I don’t know” is a valid answer.

How findings are supported

Every finding shows the public source supporting the organization-design principle and the separate GrowWithHR rule that interpreted your facts. Management-capacity questions use contextual factors rather than claiming one universal span-of-control benchmark. See methodology & sources ↗
