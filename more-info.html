Privacy and data handling

GrowWithHR uses the company and workforce information you provide to prepare the intelligence analysis you request. Limited in-progress assessment data may also be stored in your browser so you can continue on the same device and browser.

Temporary Company Workspace

When you complete a GrowWithHR intelligence analysis, reusable company information is stored in the GrowWithHR database for six months from your latest completed intelligence analysis. This lets you return with your Report ID and Workspace Recovery Code and use another GrowWithHR intelligence engine without entering the same company information again.

GrowWithHR emails the workspace email address approximately seven days before the scheduled deletion date. On the scheduled deletion date, the reusable company information is deleted and GrowWithHR sends a deletion-confirmation email. You can also request earlier deletion from a recovered Company Workspace.

The six-month period is a GrowWithHR product retention policy. It is not presented as a statutory retention requirement.

What may remain after Company Workspace deletion

Downloaded reports remain with you. GrowWithHR may retain minimal non-content Report ID and operational records where needed for report identity, security, fraud prevention, system integrity or legal obligations. Those records are separate from the reusable Company Workspace and are not intended to reconstruct the deleted company-data payload.

Email and report delivery

When you request email delivery, recipient details, the information needed to prepare the report, report data and the generated PDF are processed by the GrowWithHR backend and Gmail API. Sent emails and attachments may remain in the connected Gmail account according to that account's retention settings.

  • We collect and process information needed to provide the requested GrowWithHR intelligence and continuity experience.
  • Reusable Company Workspace data is encrypted before database storage and is not intended to be directly accessible from the browser database client.
  • Keep your Workspace Recovery Code private. A Report ID by itself is not intended to unlock reusable company information.
  • We do not sell company assessment information.
  • Do not enter confidential employee-level medical, payroll, disciplinary, performance or other sensitive personal information into GrowWithHR.
  • If a future feature stores uploaded documents or private Company RAG content, its retention and deletion controls must follow the same disclosed Company Workspace lifecycle before release.
