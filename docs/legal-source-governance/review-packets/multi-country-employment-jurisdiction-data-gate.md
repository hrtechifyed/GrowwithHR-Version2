## Handoff authority

The project handoff places Multi-country Employment outside the selected scope until a country pair and cross-border data approval are chosen. The repository readiness register likewise classifies the feature as `outside-current-india-law-scope` and requires a selected country pair plus specialist jurisdictional approval before any product rule or source pack.

Wave 5M therefore implements a stop condition, not a legal feature.

## Current India-side research leads

These public official materials are recorded only to define future research dimensions. They are **not** controlled runtime sources and do not create an India-versus-foreign-country source pack.

1. **Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 — India Code.** Section 16 concerns processing of personal data outside India when in force. India Code places sections 3–17 in the eighteen-month commencement tranche from 13 November 2025. As of 10 August 2026, Wave 5M must not represent section 16 as an operative transfer rule.
2. **Digital Personal Data Protection Rules, 2025 — MeitY.** Current rules and enforcement-timeline materials must be re-verified at the date a country pair is selected. Registration here is research context only, not transfer approval.
3. **EPFO International Worker / SSA guidance.** EPFO identifies multiple bilateral Social Security Agreements, with country-specific detachment and certificate-of-coverage treatment. A future pack must acquire the exact agreement and administrative arrangement for the selected pair or expressly record that no applicable agreement exists.
4. **Income Tax Act, 2025 non-resident guidance.** Current Income Tax Department guidance states that the 2025 Act governs tax years beginning on or after 1 April 2026. Tax residency, withholding, treaty relief and permanent-establishment consequences remain fact- and treaty-specific.
5. **Immigration and Foreigners Act, 2025 — India Code.** The Act is in force from 1 September 2025 and provides the current Indian statutory frame for visa/foreigner matters. No work-authorisation route may be selected without exact current rules/instructions and the second jurisdiction.

## Required pair-specific research before product capture

The selected pair must be researched as one bounded operating model, including:

- employing entity, payroll entity, secondment and local-registration model;
- mandatory employment law and local worker protections in both jurisdictions;
- immigration, visa, work authorisation and foreigner-registration requirements;
- individual/employer tax, residence, withholding, treaty and permanent-establishment analysis;
- social security, bilateral SSA, certificate-of-coverage and benefit-coordination treatment;
- payroll, currency, remittance and local-reporting controls;
- worker classification, agency, EOR/PEO and contractor boundaries;
- termination, collective rights, mandatory process and dispute forum;
- privacy, controller/processor roles, cross-border transfer/localisation and data-subject rights;
- security, access, retention, deletion, incident and vendor/subprocessor controls.

## Hard approval gates

No assessment fact, deterministic rule, source chunk, browser panel, provider route or runtime catalogue may be created until all of the following are explicitly resolved:

1. exactly one country pair is selected;
2. the employing/payroll/assignment operating model is defined;
3. qualified employment-law approval exists for both jurisdictions and their interaction;
4. legal/privacy/security approval exists for the proposed cross-border employee-data flow;
5. exact current immigration/work-authorisation sources are acquired;
6. exact current tax/treaty/payroll/PE sources are acquired;
7. exact SSA/administrative-arrangement sources are acquired or non-applicability is affirmatively established by qualified review;
8. exact employment-law sources for both jurisdictions are fingerprinted and section-mapped;
9. a privacy-safe organisation-level Assessment Fact Contract is separately approved.

## Prohibited product data while blocked

Wave 5M must not collect, send to a provider, persist or analyse employee identities, contact/address data, nationality/citizenship, passports, visas, immigration identifiers, tax IDs, residency-day counts, travel itineraries, UAN/social-security account numbers, certificate-of-coverage bodies, payroll/wages/compensation, bank/remittance data, medical/injury/death information, dependant/nominee data, agreement bodies, termination/dispute/case content, precise movement/location history, identifiable transfer logs or evidence bodies.

## Deliberately not decided

Wave 5M does not decide applicable employment law, work authorisation, tax residence, treaty relief, permanent establishment, payroll withholding, social-security coverage, certificate-of-coverage eligibility, worker classification, EOR/PEO legality, termination rights, dispute forum, cross-border data-transfer legality, localisation, retention, breach response or remedy.
