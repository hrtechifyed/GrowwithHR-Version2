# GrowWithHR v0.20.1 — Contextual Report Intelligence Hotfix

Status: **implementation under review**  
Baseline: `v0.20.0`  
Public assessment route: unchanged

## Problem corrected

The final PDF assembler defaulted to both Light and Dark editions when the selected theme was not forwarded to it. The same assembler rebuilt the report without the established cover/closing logo and replaced the footer area with page numbers only. It also exposed internal report-assembly language, empty zero-count status panels, raw snapshot identifiers and generic missing-question coverage that was not sufficiently filtered by organisation context.

The assessment also relied too heavily on universal workforce questions and a small set of industry profiles. That could expose manufacturing, power, worker-type or shift questions to organisations whose sector and activities did not make those questions relevant.

## Behaviour after this change

- Light generates one Light PDF.
- Dark generates one Dark PDF.
- Both generates two PDFs only when explicitly selected.
- The HRTechify logo appears on the cover and closing page.
- Every page receives a branded confidentiality footer and page number.
- Empty legal-status groups are omitted.
- Internal snapshot identifiers remain available to the runtime for change comparison but are not shown as unexplained customer-facing text.
- Internal layout explanations and raw `A#: answered x of y` statements are removed.
- Report questions are limited to the laws and operating conditions relevant to the recorded organisation profile.
- Factory, power, production-worker and standing-orders questions appear only in a manufacturing or industrial context.
- Contract-labour questions appear only when contract labour or agency-supplied people are reported.
- ESI and statutory-bonus eligibility questions are not requested below their usual workforce gates.
- A One Person Company first answers whether anyone other than the owner/director works with it. Owner-only OPCs do not receive workforce, payroll, electricity, factory, shift or sector follow-up questions.

## All-sector intelligence

Question selection now combines legal structure, workforce presence, sector, worker types, locations, operating activities, shifts and delivery model. Sector is a starting signal, not a legal conclusion.

Supported contextual profiles include:

- software, SaaS and technology;
- BPO, ITES, contact centres and shared services;
- professional services, consulting and agencies;
- retail and e-commerce;
- hospitality, restaurants and travel;
- healthcare and life sciences;
- education and training;
- logistics, warehousing and transportation;
- construction and real estate;
- banking, financial services, insurance and fintech;
- manufacturing, factories and industrial operations;
- media, creative and entertainment;
- non-profits and social enterprises;
- mixed or unclassified businesses using activity-led follow-up questions.

Examples of contextual behaviour:

- software and consulting profiles focus on client-site work, overseas staff and outsourced operations rather than factory questions;
- retail and e-commerce profiles can ask about stores, warehouses, shifts, seasonal people and multiple establishments;
- hospitality and healthcare profiles ask about continuous or night operations only when those conditions are reported;
- logistics profiles can ask about warehouses, operational workers, shifts and agency labour;
- construction profiles can ask about project sites, temporary workforces and labour contractors;
- financial-services profiles do not receive factory or production questions;
- unknown sectors begin with neutral operating-activity questions and reveal only the relevant follow-ups.

A hidden or suppressed question is not counted as unanswered, does not reduce input coverage and must not produce a missing-information action in the report.

## Protected boundaries

- No database, cloud persistence, authentication or evidence upload is introduced.
- Existing assessment, report, email and delivery storage keys remain unchanged.
- The stable public route remains `/analyze-company.html`.
- The change does not represent legal certification and retains the current advisory disclaimer.

## Validation

- browser verification that owner-only non-manufacturing OPC reports exclude factory and workforce questions;
- browser verification that major sector families receive different relevant question sets;
- browser verification that unknown sectors use activity-led questions and that stale irrelevant answers are neutralised before report generation;
