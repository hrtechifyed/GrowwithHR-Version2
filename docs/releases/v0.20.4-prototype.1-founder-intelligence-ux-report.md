# GrowWithHR v0.20.4-prototype.1 — Founder Intelligence, Guided Assessment and Report Parity

**Release date:** 13 August 2026  
**Release type:** GitHub Pre-release / research-grade prototype  
**Tag:** `v0.20.4-prototype.1`  
**Application version:** `0.20.4-prototype.1`  
**Production legal certification:** not claimed

## Release summary

GrowWithHR v0.20.4-prototype.1 extends the deterministic-first prototype into a more founder-operable compliance workflow without changing legal applicability authority.

The release adds:

- stable founder obligation objects and deterministic next-action ordering;
- missing-company-fact resolution with preview before a revised snapshot;
- scenario comparison and scale-trigger framing;
- durable server-issued Report IDs with lineage to the prior report when a revised report is generated;
- founder web/PDF parity for obligation state, actions, missing facts and scale triggers;
- governed Legal RAG explanation only after the deterministic finding is fixed;
- a guided searchable industry combobox, including `HR Consulting` mapped to the existing deterministic `Consulting & Professional Services` profile;
- removal of the required “Where would guidance be most useful right now?” priority-selection gate while retaining the People/HR support context needed by the assessment; and
- a responsive full-width founder report plus a 178 mm usable A4 jsPDF content area with 16 mm side margins.

The release remains a research prototype. It does not provide a legal opinion or certify compliance.

## Deterministic authority

The execution boundary remains:

```text
organisation facts
→ deterministic applicability rules
→ fixed finding / missing facts / reason
→ governed explanation and presentation
```

The deterministic engine remains the only applicability authority. Legal RAG/provider output remains post-decision and explanation-only. It cannot invent company facts, change the fixed status, select another legal family or certify compliance.

## Founder workflow included in this release

A founder can:

1. enter company facts through the assessment;
2. use progressive industry search rather than a long native dropdown;
3. review fixed obligation states and ordered next actions;
4. see unresolved company facts separately from fixed findings;
5. preview how an explicit missing-fact answer changes the deterministic assessment;
6. generate a revised report with a fresh server-issued Report ID and retained lineage; and
7. use scale-trigger/scenario framing to understand when a supported rule family should be reassessed.

No blended compliance score, readiness percentage or evidence certification is introduced.

## Report and PDF contract

The founder report remains one standard report edition.

The report layout uses the full responsive web container and the full usable A4 content width for the branded jsPDF writer:

```text
A4 width: 210 mm
Left margin: 16 mm
Right margin: 16 mm
Usable content width: 178 mm
```

The founder report keeps HRTechify branding and the existing prototype/legal boundaries. Report content, headings, cards and divider treatments are no longer intentionally constrained to the former narrow fixed-width web wrapper.

## Industry guidance behavior

The industry field is a searchable combobox rather than a native datalist experience.

- Typing progressively narrows relevant suggestions.
- Keyboard navigation is supported.
- `HR Consulting`, `Human Resources Consulting`, `People Consulting`, `HR Advisory` and `People Advisory` resolve to the existing deterministic `Consulting & Professional Services` profile.
- Unmatched custom industry text continues to use the established custom/Other handling rather than silently creating a new deterministic legal profile.

## Removed assessment friction

The required “Where would guidance be most useful right now?” priority-selection gate is removed from validation because it is not required to determine applicability.

The People/HR support question remains required. Presentation preferences must not alter deterministic applicability.

## Legal RAG and research standard

The effective Legal RAG runtime remains bounded by the prototype contract:

- **57 callable profiles**;
- **55 substantive profiles**;
- **2 governance fallbacks**;
- **21 active catalogues**;
- active legal catalogues remain `needs-legal-review`.

The prototype continues to use structured secondary research with controlled provenance. Exact official-source assurance remains future hardening under #143. Production-grade Legal/Privacy/RAG/Source/Security/Release certification remains future hardening under #142.

## Deliberate exclusions

This release does not add:

- production legal certification;
- exact official-source assurance for every active legal source;
- multi-country employment determination;
- safeguarding automation;
- uploaded evidence or evidence certification;
- broad new law families; or
- unreviewed compliance calculators.
