# GrowWithHR v0.20.3-prototype.1 — Governed Compliance & Legal RAG Prototype

**Release date:** 11 August 2026  
**Release type:** GitHub Pre-release / research-grade prototype

**Production legal certification:** not claimed

## Release summary

GrowWithHR v0.20.3-prototype.1 is a high-quality research prototype demonstrating a deterministic-first HR compliance engine with governed Legal RAG explanations.

The prototype is designed to help founders and HR/compliance teams understand:

- which compliance families need attention from the organisation facts supplied now;
- why the deterministic engine produced that result;
- what information is still missing;
- which governed research sources support the explanation;
- what controlled next action to take; and
- which compliance review triggers may become relevant as workforce size, locations, worker categories or operating model changes.

It is not a legal opinion or compliance certification system.

## Prototype research standard

This release uses **structured secondary research with controlled provenance** as the accepted legal/source basis.

- Secondary-research provenance is not represented as official or counsel-approved provenance.
- Exact official-file verification is supplementary assurance rather than a prototype release prerequisite.
- Draft, guidance, portal and research-only classifications remain explicit.
- Existing exact-file reconciliation evidence may remain separate from runtime source identities.
- The prototype does not claim legal certification or proof of compliance.

The Source Register also contains supplementary reconciliation evidence for 31 acquired files, including file-level SHA-256, byte length and physical page count where recorded. No runtime migration to those exact-file fingerprints is required for this prototype release.

Future exact official-source hardening remains tracked under #143. Future production-grade Legal/Privacy/RAG/Source/Security/Release certification remains tracked under #142.

## Deterministic-first Legal RAG

The execution order remains:

```text
organisation facts
→ privacy-safe fact mapping
→ versioned deterministic rule
→ fixed status/reason/missing facts/permitted source IDs
→ governed retrieval
→ retrieval fingerprint + citations
→ explanation-only provider
→ strict response validation
→ user-facing explanation and next action
```

RAG/provider output remains:

```text
usedForDecision: false
applicabilityAuthority: none
```

The provider cannot create facts, change status/reason/source scope, choose another legal family or certify compliance.

## Deliberate boundaries

### Wave 5J — Bonded and Forced Labour

Wave 5J remains governance/research-only with no substantive assessment/runtime/provider path. Live coercion, trafficking, confinement, violence, retaliation, rescue or comparable safeguarding concerns remain human-only.

### Wave 5M — Multi-country Employment

Wave 5M remains outside current release scope. There is no country pair, international assessment contract, runtime catalogue, provider/browser route or cross-border data design in this release.

## Privacy and reliability

- Legal-review panels remain explicit-submit and in-memory only.
- Feature clients use allow-listed payloads.
- Person-level identities, payroll/contribution bodies, medical/case narratives, complaint/dispute bodies, notices/orders and evidence bodies remain outside the normal provider path.
- Provider output is strictly validated and decision-changing output fails closed.
- Stable report/PDF/email contracts remain protected unless separately versioned.
