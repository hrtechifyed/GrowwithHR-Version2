Structure: compares manager capacity, hierarchy and location complexity directly against the Current scenario; it does not apply a universal span-of-control benchmark or hidden cutoff. Workforce: uses the capability-coverage input as a planning signal, not an employee score. Compliance review: identifies when changed facts justify refreshing HR Compliance Readiness; it does not determine applicable law. Cost: is calculated only from the company-supplied annual people-cost assumption.
