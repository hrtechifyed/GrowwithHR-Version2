GrowWithHR Account · Prototype

Save your thinking, not just the final report.

Real GrowWithHR analyses require a signed-in account so your assessment can save automatically, continue later and stay linked to your reports. Sample reports remain open without signing in.

Auto-save

Your progress can be saved while you work, so a thoughtful assessment does not have to be completed in one sitting.

Resume later

Return to your GrowWithHR workspace and continue the latest unfinished assessment.

Reports in one place

Completed reports stay connected to your account rather than depending on a code you have to remember.

Google or email

Use email and password, or continue with Google. Password reset is available when needed.

This branch is a working product prototype, not a production authentication rollout. The account database migration and OAuth settings must be reviewed before release.
